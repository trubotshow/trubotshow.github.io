FAQ

The app isn't working properly.

Try force-quitting and reopening the app. If that doesn't help, email us your device model, iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

How do I restore my purchases?

Tap “Restore Purchases” in the app settings. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase.

How do I get a refund?

App Store refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
