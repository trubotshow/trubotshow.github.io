Trudice — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Trudice (the "App") rolls dice. That's it. The dice are curious about your luck. The App is not curious about you. Still, here are the details.

1. Information the developer collects

None. The App has no sign-up or login, no analytics, and the developer runs no servers. The App does not connect to the internet.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • Practically nothing is saved. The App starts fresh with two dice every time.
  • Motion sensor: read only in the moment, so a shake makes the dice jump. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

3. Advertising

The App currently shows no ads. If ads are ever added in a future version, this policy will be updated first to explain what the ad provider collects and the choices you have.

4. Children

The App collects nothing from anyone, children included.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

6. Contact

Questions? Email trubotshow@gmail.com. (Developer: Trubotshow)
