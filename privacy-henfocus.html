Henfocus — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Henfocus (the "App") is a focus clock for iPhone: a hen on your table lays an egg for every six minutes you focus. It is made to keep your data where it belongs: on your phone.

1. Information the developer collects

None. The App has no sign-up or login, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers. The App does not connect to the internet.

2. What stays on your iPhone

  • Your egg record: the date and time of each egg the hen lays on the focus clock, and whether it was a golden one. This is what the egg tray calendar shows.
  • A few settings, such as the hen's colour and whether the buttons are hidden.
  • None of this leaves your iPhone, and the developer cannot see it. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

3. Permissions

The App asks for no permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or health data. While it is open it keeps the screen from turning off, like a desk clock; it goes back to your usual setting when you leave the App.

4. Purchases

The App has no in-app purchases.

5. Children

The App collects nothing from anyone, children included.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
