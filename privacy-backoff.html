Back Off — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Back Off (the "App"; called 좀만 뒤로 in Korean and ちょっと下がって in Japanese) uses your Mac's camera to notice when your head drifts toward the screen. A camera app should be clear about what it sees, so here it is.

1. Information the developer collects

None. The App has no sign-up or login, no analytics, no ads, and the developer runs no servers. The App itself does not connect to the internet.

2. The camera

  • The App looks at camera frames only to find the size and position of your face and upper body, using Apple's on-device Vision framework. Each frame is checked in memory and thrown away right away.
  • Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. No photos, no video.
  • By default the camera turns on for about two seconds once a minute. If you choose a faster interval or "Always", it stays on longer. You can pause it any time from the menu bar, which turns the camera off.

3. What stays on your Mac

  • Your "sitting straight" reference: three numbers (face size, face height and upper-body width). No image.
  • Your settings, such as how often to check and how sensitive the App is.
  • With Pro's posture history: how many seconds per hour you sat well, leaned in or were away, and how many warnings you got. Numbers only, kept on your Mac for 90 days and then deleted automatically. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Deleting the App deletes all of the above.

4. In-App Purchase

Back Off Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The App only asks the App Store, on your Mac, whether Pro has been purchased.

5. Children

The App collects nothing from anyone, children included.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
