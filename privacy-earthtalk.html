Earthtalk — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Earthtalk (the "App"; 지구말 in Korean, ちきゅうことば in Japanese) is a face-to-face translator for iPhone: voice, text and photos, in nine languages. It is made to keep your words where they belong: on your phone.

1. Information the developer collects

None. The App has no sign-up or login, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the developer runs no servers. Nothing you say, type or photograph is sent to the developer.

2. Translation

Translation is done by Apple's Translation on your iPhone. If it cannot translate something, Apple Intelligence on your iPhone translates it instead (when you have it turned on). The first time you use a language, iOS may ask to download it; you can also download languages in the App's Settings so they work without the internet.

3. Microphone and speech recognition

The App listens only while you have tapped to speak. Your speech is turned into text on your iPhone where the iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple's speech recognition does it. No recording is kept.

4. Camera and photos

On the Photo page the App reads the text the camera sees, or in a photo you choose, on your iPhone (Vision) and translates it. The last ten translated photos are remembered only while the App is open and are gone when you close it. A translated photo is saved to your photo library only when you tap to save it (the App can only add to your library, not read it).

5. What stays on your iPhone

  • The languages you chose, your settings, and the phrases you save. Deleting the App deletes all of them.
  • Conversations you translate exist only while the App is open.
  • If you choose "Similar voice", the App remembers only the pitch of your voice (a single number) so translations can be read in a voice like yours. If you choose "My voice", iOS asks before the App may use your Personal Voice.

6. Permissions

Microphone, speech recognition, camera and adding to photos are asked for only when you first use them, and can be turned off any time in iPhone Settings › Earthtalk. With any of them off, the rest of the App still works.

7. Purchases

The App has no in-app purchases.

8. Children

The App collects nothing from anyone, children included.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the App with a new effective date.
